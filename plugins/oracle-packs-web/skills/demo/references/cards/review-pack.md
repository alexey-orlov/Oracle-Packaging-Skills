# What the owner sees

**What this is.** The review round. The owner never approves silently: expect a numbered list back, expect a rebuild round, and expect corrections to arrive as a screenshot and a few words.

**What goes in it**

1. **The running thing to click through.** He reviews the live demo, never a description of it. This is the one artifact that **may be published as a claude.ai artifact** — it is synthetic and customer-free by rule; open it there and hand over the link.
2. **The frames as a contact sheet** and the value band.
3. **A TLDR of what changed** since the last cut, including the red-team delta: what got wider, and why.
4. **Every invented number, with what it stands for.** Every one — not a footnote.
5. **Everything on screen that has no counterpart in the real product**, in plain words, with the reason it was drawn that way.
6. **The decisions that remain his**, stated as open items rather than resolved ones.

**The checks**

- One rebuild round; a single-step change is the fast path — redo that step, re-capture, stop.
- Nothing internal in anything he reads: no step or test names, no file, key or script names, no A/B/C letters, no packaging vocabulary as vocabulary.
- A shared working tree and a shared preview mean another session may be editing and publishing the same thing. Expect record-section numbers to collide and publish refusals to happen: re-read, merge, republish.
- Never record an environment limitation as a conclusion. "Could not reach it" is deferred and retried, never "not found".
