# Standard extras on every sales artifact

**What this is.** Eight blocks that are not among the 12 components and are not decoration. Each appears on every sales artifact that has room for it.

1. **"Why it sells: for the partner's seller."** Partner value, distinct from customer value: the cross-sell path into a product the account already owns, the **net-new consumption** the pack creates, and the repeatability across similar accounts. On every partner-facing artifact; correctly absent from the listing and the demo, which are customer-facing.
2. **Proof block.** Logo where cleared, or the anonymized descriptor; a three-sentence story; the three stats; the caveat immediately beneath them. Status said once, in one plain word; the footnote spends its line on evidence.
3. **Solution-layers ladder.** The four-rung vendor attribution (`shared/references/anatomy/architecture.md`). Distinct from the architecture diagram, which is a data flow.
4. **CTA + contact, per channel.** The question, the offer, and the contact the channel dictates — a named person in print, a practice mailbox on the site, the packaging owner internally. Never a personal mailbox on a public page. The spec's `contacts` block carries all three so nobody picks.
5. **Disclaimer / caveat line.** Universal. A figure never ships without one; an indicative price never ships without one.
6. **Glyph legend.** Wherever a glyph appears, its key appears on the same page or slide. One system: `● ◐ ○` for feature status, `◐ ● ●● —` for tier coverage.
7. **In-scope / out-of-scope boundary.** One boundary sentence, then a numbered in-scope list and a numbered out-of-scope list. On any artifact a buyer uses to decide.
8. **Roadmap rows.** What is deliberately not in the pack today, stated positively and only where cleared — never published externally as a count or a negation.
