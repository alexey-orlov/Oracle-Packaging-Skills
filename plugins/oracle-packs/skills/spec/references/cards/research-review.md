# Your call on the research

**What this is.** The judgement calls no document answers. **At most four questions, in one call**, titled `Your call on the research · n of N · <name>`. Ask only what the research genuinely raised: where it is confident and the owner has no stake, state the answer and move on.

**Candidates, in priority order** — take the live ones:

- **Which industries we really sell into** — which are genuinely different businesses, which the same thing renamed? *Changes:* what the pack claims and what the deck leads with.
- **Which differences between industries are real** — the research listed what makes each different; which are real and which padding? *Changes:* the survivors become each industry's "what matters here" line.
- **What happens when things go wrong, and in which package** — per step: the proof of value handles it · it waits for Integration · we say plainly we do not cover it. *Changes:* the proof's scope and the "not in scope" lines documents print.
- **How broad the pack should be** — this breadth · one step broader (name the boundary that keeps it honest) · one step narrower (name what we drop).
- **What we do not cover that we should** — per vendor gap: build it now · roadmap · leave it out; and for each thing the engagement left out, whether the pack leaves it out too.

**Checks this stage must pass**

1. Four questions maximum, one call; nothing merged into the story pick.
2. Every question restates what it refers to in one plain sentence.
3. Each says what it changes about the pack, and the default if skipped.
4. Options are outcomes for the pack, never states of the method.

**Fills:** `verticals[]`, `workflow.steps[].failure_path`, `capabilities[]`, the scope lines.
