# The buyer and the name

**What this is.** What every story candidate's buyer line and name must pass, beside cards `story` and `one-liner`.

**Who buys it**: one line with the buyer's role, the company and the pain, from the customer's side: a shape of operation, not an industry list. The partner's product they run may qualify the line, never define it. No headcount threshold without a source. It reads as the feature list's "For …" line and the site's "Where it applies"; buyer roles, qualifying signals and disqualifiers stay internal.

**The name**: noun-led, the thing and not the job ("Damage assessment", never "Repair or replace"); plain enough that an Oracle rep needs no briefing and a buyer would search for it; no packaging vocabulary, counts, customer name or internal acronym; a vendor trademark in it has its usage rules checked. Each audience's variant and casing is derived once, by rule (`shared/references/naming-and-clearance.md`).

**Good.** `Workforce Optimization` (site), `Workforce Optimization App` (internal), `Workforce optimization` (print).

**Fills:** `icp.line`, `icp.buyer_roles`, `icp.qualifying_signals`, `icp.disqualifiers`, `meta.name`, `meta.name_variants`.
