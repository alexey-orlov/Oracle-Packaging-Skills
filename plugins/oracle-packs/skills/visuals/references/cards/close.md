# Closing

**What this is.** The list the owner is left with: what was chosen, and what is still open.

**The checks**

1. One line per picture chosen, saying what it is; then where the files are, and that the record of the licence for each is kept beside them.
2. **Every slot with no choice is named plainly**, as what it is on the slide — "the photograph for the right-hand side of the before-and-after slide has nothing you liked yet; the deck will show an empty frame there until it does."
3. Each of those says **what would unblock it**: different words to search for, or a key for a picture library this machine cannot reach.
4. A source that could not be reached is reported as **deferred**, with what it needs — never as "nothing found".
5. That list is the open items, said plainly and not buried at the end of a paragraph.
6. No slot was filled with a stand-in to make the list shorter.
7. The chosen pictures and the brief were saved to the shared repo first (the spec skill's card `save`), so a colleague's build finds them; a save that failed is an open item too.

**Good.** "Three of your four industries have an icon. Transport has none yet — tell me a word or two closer to what you'd like to see and I'll search again."

**Bad.** "`verticals[2].icon`: no match, slot deferred pending an access key."

**Reads:** what the record step wrote. **Writes:** nothing.
