# The one-pager's copy, and what the owner gets

**What this is.** What the editorial pass checks on the one-pager beyond card `editorial`, and what its review pack and delivery add.

**Checks**

1. The page alternates paragraph, bullets, chips, figures and table: no three same-shape blocks in a row.
2. The proof strip speaks about the customer's business, their problem and what changed for them, never the engagement's mechanics (weeks, phases, contract status, "a proof of value" as a noun phrase), which belong to the caveat or the packages table.
3. The page reads as the whole offering, not as the delivered case with the names changed.
4. "Why it sells" is written for the partner's seller, in one card, and appears on no customer-facing artifact.
5. The closing block carries this audience's contact; an address is a link, never a filled button.
6. The infrastructure row says "indicative" where the spec does.

**The review pack adds** which blocks are at or over their length and by how much, by the block's name on the page.

**Delivery.** The PDF and its editable HTML twin: `<Pack name> - Sales one-pager - Oracle.pdf` and `.html`. The HTML ships on purpose: it is the source, the PDF a render of it.

**Good.** "Here is the page. The packages table is the fullest block and the proof story is at its limit; the infrastructure price is marked indicative because the brief marks it so."
**Bad.** "Built the one-pager, lint clean, 2 blocks OVER budget — approve?"

**Reads:** `packages.why_it_sells_for_the_partner[]`, `one_pager.cta`, `contacts.<channel>`, `kpis[].kind`, `kpis[].caveat`.
