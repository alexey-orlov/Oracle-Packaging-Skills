# What the owner sees

**What this is.** The demo's review round, on top of the shared review protocol: what the owner gets beyond it.

**What goes in it**

1. **The running walkthrough to click through**, published as a claude.ai artifact, with its link. It is the one artifact that may be, because it is synthetic and customer-free by rule.
2. **The frames as a contact sheet**, and the value band.
3. **What changed since the last cut**, including the red-team delta: what got wider, and why.
4. **Every invented number, with what it stands for**: every one, not a footnote.
5. **Everything on screen with no counterpart in the real product**, in plain words, with the reason it was drawn that way.

**The checks**

- A single-step change is the fast path: redo that step, re-capture, stop.
- No A/B/C letters in anything the owner reads.
- A shared working tree and a shared preview mean another session may be editing and publishing the same thing: expect record-section numbers to collide and publish refusals to happen; re-read, merge, republish.
- An environment limitation is never a conclusion: "could not reach it" is deferred and retried, never "not found".
