Oracle accelerator packs · packaging approach

How a pack gets made

One delivered engagement becomes a repeatable pack. One signed-off spec feeds every artifact.

01

Collect the inputs

SoW, PoC deck, demo recording, feature lists, call transcripts, sibling packs.

02

Agent asks

A fixed question list: inputs, the delivered case, verticals, clearance of customer name and figures, PoV constraints. It skips whatever the context already answers.

03

Agent researches

  • The general workflow for the job across industries, and where the delivered case differs.
  • Vendor and competitor capability taxonomies, then the gap check.
  • Per step: what matters, what fails, which features are customer, engine, use case or industry specific.

Out: a structured research TLDR

04

You decide

Pick one of the proposed name, one-liner and problem-to-solution options, or send it back for more research. Then sign off the 12 shared components one at a time, each with a brief TLDR, its grounding and a choice. Confirm the whole brief.

05

Artifacts get built

In order, with one review pause after each: feature list, sales deck, sales one-pager, executive summary, mini-site listing, interactive demo.
