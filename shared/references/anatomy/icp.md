# Component 3 — target ICP

**What this is.** One line naming who buys, at what kind of company, with what pain — written from the **customer's** side, not the seller's. Buyer roles, qualifying signals and disqualifiers support it in the spec and reach internal artifacts only.

**Checks the draft must pass**

1. It names a **shape of operation**, not an industry list (that is component 5) and not a product prerequisite.
2. It is customer ICP, never **seller** ICP. "Fits any customer with a heavy, centralized dispatch routine, on Oracle Field Service" gates the ICP on owning a specific product — that is a partner-value statement and belongs in "Why it sells".
3. No headcount threshold with no source behind it.
4. It reads as one line, both on the feature list's "For …" line and on the site's "Where it applies".

**Good.** *"Any mobile field force planned against skills, availability and geography."* · *"Any business that needs to turn market and customer developments into pursuable opportunities across its account base, quickly."*

**Bad.** Four industry nouns. "Enterprises with more than 500 field staff", with nothing behind the number.

**Fills:** `icp.line`, `icp.buyer_roles`, `icp.qualifying_signals`, `icp.disqualifiers`.
